New order

DAY validity. Pending orders expire at the end of the trading day. Quotes are snapshots, not a streaming feed. Selling may open a short position unless you use the position’s Exit button.

Prices refer to this exact futures/options contract, not the underlying index. Limit buys can execute at or below your limit; the entry trigger only activates a stop-limit entry. Enter one exit distance per unit: after a verified fill, target and initial stop are calculated the same distance above and below the average fill rounded to the nearest tradable tick (reversed for sells). Gross target/loss before costs is distance × filled units; tick rounding, fees and execution can change the result. Existing positions/orders must be cleared before managed entry.

One protective stop-limit is placed after verified entry completion. A partial entry causes cancellation of the remainder first. Target converts that same exit to a limit at fresh bid minus offset (sell) or ask plus offset (buy). Trailing follows the best observed executable price and never loosens the stop. Gaps may leave limits unfilled. The server must remain running and logged in; the browser may be closed. Management ends at 15:25 IST and must be checked in Kotak. Stop/target prices are not guaranteed.

Managed strategies

Armed strategies are monitored server-side every 5 seconds. Disarm prevents further automatic requests; broker orders and positions remain active. A request already sent may still complete. Suspended strategies need a fresh review to resume. Use Kotak to handle active orders if this app is unavailable.
